# ScreenSense

Move your monitor, look at it, done. ScreenSense is a tiny macOS menu-bar app that rearranges your displays to match where your monitor physically sits, using your laptop camera to see which way you turn your head.

## Features

- **Hands-free layout**: look around your monitor and your displays rearrange to match
- **9 snap spots**: left, right or above the laptop, each at top/middle/bottom (or left/center/right)
- **Edge alignment**: line up top/bottom or left/right edges
- **Manual placement**: pick any layout from the menu, no camera needed
- **Lightweight and private**: under 1 MB, no dependencies, the camera runs for about 6 seconds, nothing is recorded or uploaded

## How to Use

Press **⌃⌥⌘M** or click the menu-bar icon → **Arrange Displays**.

1. **Tink**: turn your head around the monitor's four corners
2. **Pop**: look at the middle of your laptop screen
3. **Glass**: done

Turn your head, not just your eyes. Use **Place Monitor** in the menu to pick a layout directly.

## Install

1. Download `ScreenSense.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest)
2. Unzip it and drag **ScreenSense** into your Applications folder
3. Open it. The first time, macOS blocks it: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**

Requires macOS 12+ and a MacBook with the lid open plus one external monitor.
